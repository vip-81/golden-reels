Rules without the fine print

How to play both games

Every round is resolved instantly in your browser. The controls stay the same on desktop and mobile: choose a bet, act, and read the status line for the result.

01

Three-reel slots

  1. Choose a stake. Bets are 10, 25, 50, or 100 credits per spin.
  2. Press Spin. The stake is deducted immediately, then each reel stops on one of six symbols.
  3. Read the paytable. Three matching symbols pay a multiplier. Exactly two cherries pay 1×. Everything else is a loss.
  4. Keep playing or reset. Your balance is saved in this browser. Reset bankroll restores 1,000 credits at any time.

The demo's published slot paytable has an approximate return to player of 90.3% across many spins. Short sessions can vary widely.

02

Six-deck blackjack

  1. Deal. You and the dealer each get two cards. The dealer's second card stays face down.
  2. Hit or stand. Hit takes another card; stand keeps your total and reveals the dealer's hand.
  3. Double once. On your first two cards, double your bet, take exactly one more card, and end your turn.
  4. Compare totals. The dealer draws to 17 and stands on all 17. A natural blackjack pays 3:2; a tie pushes your stake back.

Aces count as 11 unless that would bust the hand, in which case they count as 1. There is no split, insurance, or surrender in this lightweight version.

How the 90.3% figure is calculated

Each reel is independently uniform across six symbols. A specific three-of-a-kind has a 1 in 216 chance, so the six triple payouts contribute (80 + 40 + 25 + 15 + 12 + 8) ÷ 216 = 0.8333. Exactly two cherries has a probability of 15 in 216 and pays 1×, adding 0.0694. The combined expected return is 0.9028, or about 90.3%. This is a mathematical property of the demo paytable, not a promise about any individual session.

Randomness and privacy

How the games stay honest

No server decides your result. The slot reels and the blackjack shoe are generated by local JavaScript each time you play, using the browser's cryptographic random number generator.

Unbiased reel sampling

Each reel stop comes from crypto.getRandomValues with rejection sampling, avoiding the modulo bias that a naive random % 6 can introduce.

Six-deck blackjack shoe

Cards are shuffled with a crypto-backed Fisher–Yates shuffle. The shoe is rebuilt when fewer than 20 cards remain. Dealer stands on all 17, including soft 17.

No tracking scripts

The page loads only its own CSS, JavaScript, icons, and manifest. There are no analytics, advertising, social, or font requests. Your balance is stored only in localStorage.

Accessible by design

Semantic landmarks, visible focus styles, 44px+ controls, reduced-motion support, high-contrast colors, and live status messages make the game usable with a keyboard or screen reader.
